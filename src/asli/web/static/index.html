Asli Is this real?

Not sure if it's real? Ask Asli.

Paste a message, upload a screenshot, or enter a link. Asli checks its claims on the live web and shows you the evidence.

Don't include OTPs, passwords or card numbers. Your message is read by an AI model, and its claims are searched on Google through SerpApi.

Try an example
  1. Reads the claimsWho it says it's from, the number, the link, the price, the job.
  2. Checks them liveGoogle Search, News, Lens, Shopping, Jobs and Maps, through SerpApi.
  3. Shows the evidenceEvery warning links to its source. The score is rules, not an AI's guess.
